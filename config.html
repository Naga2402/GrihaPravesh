Date, time & place

Pick the ceremony date and time. The weekday, month, day, year and time on the card and poster update in both తెలుగు and English, and the countdown follows it. You can still change the wording below.

Guest QR codes open this link plus the guest's name. The map link is used by the Directions button and, if you turn it on under Venue & hosts, by a QR code in place of the address. Leave the site link empty while you test on this computer.

Invitation text

Every word on the door, card and poster. Fill in both languages, then choose which one guests see first. Tip: tap any text in the preview to jump to its box.

Order of the day

Shown as a timeline under the card. Add each part of the day with its time, in both languages. Leave the list empty to hide it.

    Blessings from the family

    A photo and a few short messages that gently rotate under the card. Write one message per line.

    Optional: a family photo, or of the new home. It is shown in a gold ring and shrunk automatically to keep the invitation light.

    Music & poster look

    Soft music starts when the guest lights the diya, with a mute button on the card. The built-in tune is a veena-style melody in raga Mohanam, made in the browser, so no file is needed.

    Play music when the door opensGuests can mute it any time.

    The list shows the songs in the project's assets/audio folder. To add one, copy the MP3 there and reload this page. On a hosted site, also add its file name to assets/audio/tracks.json.

    Publish

    Your edits are saved on this device as you type. To show them to guests, export config.js, replace the file in the project folder, and upload the folder again.
